unverified here while being tested -- the count is a floor, not a ceiling.

## 6. Known limits

- Detection reports what a repository uses; the language engines still gate their framework models on their own imports. The registry does not yet feed them.
- Template files (Razor, Blade, Twig, ERB) are not parsed by the AST engines; their findings come from the regex layer.
- A missing CSRF token or authorization check cannot be told apart from middleware installed in another file; only explicit "switched off" patterns are reported.
- Sanitizers clear only the sink classes they neutralise (`taint/sanitizers.ts`); validation (a bound or allow-list check) clears through guards, not by name.
